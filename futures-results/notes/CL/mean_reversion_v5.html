Same test as the equity indexes, on crude oil. Same 41,472-version v5 grid and funnel, with validation needing ≥ 40 trades. Two differences:

  • Session: the crude day session, 09:00–14:30 New York (around the 14:30 settlement), instead of 09:30–16:00. A 1-hour day is 5½ bars, the last a half bar.
  • No cross-market check, since no related market (such as Brent) is in the data yet.

CL rolls monthly (106 rolls, back-adjusted). The front-month series never went negative in April 2020; its low was $6.50. Databento's data is partly missing on 2020-02-27 and 2020-06-30, and 2020-02-28 is missing entirely, the same gaps as YM.

15-minute bars work best here, unlike the equity indexes. On 2018–2022, 43% of 15-minute versions made money, against 24% on 1-hour and 15% on 5-minute bars. 71 of the 75 finalists are 15-minute. Up-bar confirmation and a 1 × ATR stop helped, and volatility length 20 was the strongest.

2018–2022 looked great, mostly because of 2020–2022. The finalists' median 2018–2022 return was +98% (Sharpe 0.85), but nearly all of it came from the volatile 2020–2022 period (median +26%, +17% and +15%, against +5% and +2% in 2018–2019). Their median drawdown was −17%, deeper than any equity index.

Most finalists failed on 2023–2024. Only 7 of 75 passed (4 groups). The median finalist's 2023–2024 Sharpe was −0.53, and only 15 of 75 made money. 8 also broke the −30% drawdown limit. The 2018–2022 ranking pointed the wrong way (rank correlation −0.19).

The passers are one family. All 7 use entry −2, hold 5, up-bar confirmation and a stop. Five are 15-minute SMA 30 with volatility 20; the best was #53 (2 × ATR stop: Sharpe 0.80, +14.6%, 172 trades, drawdown −10%). The others are 15-minute SMA 40/30 (Sharpe 0.35–0.50) and one 1-hour SMA 20/10 (0.65, +11.4%).

They beat holding crude, which went nowhere. Holding CL (rolled, unlevered) made −0.4% over 2023–2024 (Sharpe 0.15, drawdown −26%), and lost money with a −91% drawdown over 2018–2022. The passers made +4.5% to +14.6% with −7% to −11% drawdowns. That's the first market where the passers beat holding the market, but CL was flat to down here.

Mixed stability picture. Around the top-ranked passer (#29: 15-minute SMA 30, volatility 20, 1 × ATR stop), average length 40 scores 0.59–0.92 across almost every volatility length. Average 30 is weaker, and average 20 and 60–80 are patchy. So there's a plateau, but at average 40 rather than at the passers' 30.

Fresh data. No strategy had been tested on CL before, so 2023–2024 is close to a clean check. The grid and rules were tuned on stock-index results, a different asset class. The 2025+ holdout has not been run.
