CL round 3, 15-minute bars only. 16,128 versions, specified by you after reading CL v5 and v6:

  • SMA and EMA; averages 30, 40–60 in steps of 5, 70 and 80; volatility 15/18/20/22/25/30/75.
  • Entry −2/−2.5/−3/−3.5, exit −1/0/+0.5, hold 3/5/8, up-bar confirmation, stop none/1×/1.5×/2× ATR.

Same data and rules as CL v6. CL 2023–2024 is largely in-sample here, since the v5 and v6 results shaped the grid.

On 2018–2022 the grid looks strong. 70% of versions made money (median Sharpe 0.35), and 1,925 cleared the profit gate.

  • A stop matters most: 1× and 1.5× ATR scored median Sharpe 0.46–0.49, 2× 0.32, and no stop only 0.08.
  • Volatility 20–22 was best (0.52–0.65), and 75 was the only negative length (−0.13). Average 30 was weak (0.11); 40–70 were all similar (0.34–0.43).
  • EMA beat SMA slightly (0.40 against 0.31). Entry, exit and hold made little difference.
  • 1,905 of the 1,925 passed the stable-settings test.

But only 11 of 150 finalists passed 2023–2024 (6 of 76 groups), and weakly. Just 32 of 150 made money, and the median finalist's Sharpe was −0.36. 8 failed on drawdown worse than −30%.

  • All 11 passers use a stop (9 at 1.5×, 2 at 1×) and nearly all hold 5. The median passer made +5.7%, Sharpe 0.40, drawdown −9.0%, 101 trades.
  • The best was #89 (SMA 30/18, entry −2.5, exit −1, hold 5, 1.5× stop): Sharpe 0.77, +10.1%, 94 trades. #87 and #71 (SMA 30/20, entry −2) made about +9% on 181 trades each, Sharpe 0.51–0.53.

The 2018–2022 ranking didn't predict 2023–2024 (rank correlation −0.14). The top two failed; #3 and #4 passed with Sharpe 0.38–0.42.

Not better than luck. The median finalist beat 37% of random-entry runs on Sharpe and the best passer 89%. Holding CL lost 0.4% over 2023–2024 (Sharpe 0.15), so the passers did beat simply holding.
