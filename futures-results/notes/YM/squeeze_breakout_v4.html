YM squeeze round 4, 15-minute bars. 3,840 versions, specified after reading the ES and NQ squeeze rounds' 2018–2022, 2023–2024 and 2025+ results. This is the first squeeze run on YM:

  • Squeeze length 15/25/35/45 bars (35 and 45 reach into the previous session), Keltner width 1.5/2/2.5/3/3.5 ATR, at least 1/4 squeeze bars in a row.
  • Close or wick entry; the box stays live 1/4 bars; any box width.
  • Stop at the box or 1.5 ATR; target 1R/2.5R/none; long or both ways; EMA(50) filter on/off.

2023–2024 is a clean test on YM. No squeeze strategy had been scored on YM before this round. The 2025+ holdout is untouched.

The squeeze is much weaker on YM than on ES or NQ. Only 55% of versions made money on 2018–2022 (median Sharpe 0.07), and just 140 of 3,840 cleared the profit gate (Sharpe ≥ 0.75), against 18–19% of the grid on NQ. All 140 passed the stability checks. What helped:

  • A wick entry (65% profitable) over a close entry (45%).
  • A Keltner width of 1.5–2 ATR (66–69% profitable, against 42–50% for 2.5 and wider), and longer squeezes (35/45 bars: 64–66%, against 43–47% for 15/25).
  • Unlike ES and NQ, the EMA(50) filter hurt: 46% profitable with it, 64% without. Stop, target, setup expiry, direction and minimum squeeze bars made little difference.

59 finalists from 35 groups, all of which survived 1.5× costs.

37 of 59 passed 2023–2024, from 21 groups. The median passer made +5.4% over two years at Sharpe 0.88, profit factor 1.21, drawdown −2.6%. Across all 59 the median was +3.8%, Sharpe 0.59, profit factor 1.15. 8 finalists had a 2023–2024 Sharpe of 1.0 or more; the best was 1.37.

  • Holding YM made +19% (Sharpe 0.82, drawdown −10%).
  • Long-only carried the round: 35 of 41 long-only finalists passed (median Sharpe 0.79), against 2 of 18 two-way ones (0.16). Wick entries passed 36 of 49 times, close entries 1 of 10.
  • Lengths 15 and 25 held up (32 of 39 passed); the longer squeezes that led on 2018–2022 mostly failed (5 of 20). The 2018–2022 ranking said little about 2023–2024 (rank correlation 0.15).

Better than random, but not by much. The median finalist beat 80% of random-entry runs on Sharpe; 6 beat 95% and none beat 99%.
