What changed from YM v5. Only the grid, rewritten after YM v5's results: 15-minute and 1-hour bars only, average length 20–100, volatility length 10–25, entry −1.75 to −3.5, exit −1 to +1, max hold 5/10/15, confirmation on or off, and stop none or 0.75–2 × ATR. That makes 72,000 versions. The funnel is unchanged.

Read this first: 2023–2024 is no longer a fair test for YM. The grid was chosen after reading YM v5's 2023–2024 results, so those years are partly in-sample. Only the 2025+ holdout is untouched.

A bigger grid found better-looking 2018–2022 results that didn't hold up. 860 versions cleared the profit test (v5: 127), and 267 finalists from 146 groups went to validation. Only 41 passed 2023–2024 (15%, against 9 of 61 in v5), from 30 groups. The median finalist lost money in 2023–2024 (Sharpe −0.20), and only 95 of 267 were profitable.

The best 2018–2022 versions did worst on new data. The rank correlation between 2018–2022 and 2023–2024 Sharpe was −0.12. The top-ranked passers had 2018–2022 Sharpe 0.9–1.2 and made only 0.31–0.57 on 2023–2024, at +1.6% to +4.0%. Most of these are new settings this grid added, mainly entry −1.75 with long averages (80–100) and tight stops.

The best 2023–2024 results are the v5 survivors again. The top two were #255 (1-hour SMA 50, volatility 20, entry −2, exit −0.5, hold 10, up bar, no stop: Sharpe 1.20, +10.3%), which is exactly v5's best passer, and #179 (1-hour EMA 80/10, entry −2.5: 0.88, +7.0%), also from v5. Both were ranked low on 2018–2022 here. The new grid added nothing that beat them.

A plateau on old data isn't enough. Around the top-ranked finalist (#25: 1-hour EMA 80, volatility 15, entry −1.75, up bar, 0.75 × ATR stop), almost every average and volatility combination scored 0.66–1.15 on 2018–2022. That's a broad plateau, yet it made only 0.38 on 2023–2024. The stability tests only check robustness inside 2018–2022; they can't detect the market changing afterwards.

What the ablation shows. On 2018–2022, 1-hour beat 15-minute (55% against 35% profitable), up-bar confirmation clearly helped (53% against 37%), volatility 15–20 beat 10 and 25, and deeper entries (−3, −3.5) beat −1.75. The finalists leaned the other way (92 of 267 at entry −1.75), because the shallow entry trades more often and so clears the trade-count gates more easily.

Compared with holding YM. Holding YM made +19% over 2023–2024 (Sharpe 0.82). The median passer made +3.2% (Sharpe 0.52) with a −3.3% drawdown, in the market about 8.5% of the time. The luck check's best score was 0.66 against a 0.95 pass mark (unreviewed), and that doesn't count the earlier runs.
