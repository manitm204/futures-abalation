Same test as ES and NQ v5. Same 41,472-version grid and funnel, validation needing ≥ 40 trades, cross-market check on ES. Note: Databento's YM data is partly missing on three 2018–2022 sessions (2020-02-27, 2020-02-28 and 2020-06-30). Indicators run straight through those gaps.

1-hour bars work best here of any market so far. On 2018–2022, 62% of 1-hour versions made money (median Sharpe 0.17), against 23% on 15-minute and 7% on 5-minute bars (NQ 1-hour: 40%, ES: 45%). 127 versions cleared the profit test and 61 finalists from 37 groups went to validation.

9 of 61 passed 2023–2024, from 7 groups, but the finalists as a whole faded. The median finalist's 2023–2024 Sharpe was −0.08, and only 25 of 61 made money. 7 of the 9 passers are 1-hour. The best were #56 (1-hour SMA 50, volatility 20, entry −2, exit −0.5, hold 10, up bar, no stop: Sharpe 1.20, +10.3%, 46 trades), #57 (1-hour SMA 60/20, 2 × ATR stop: 0.92, +5.2%, 82 trades) and #37 (1-hour EMA 80/10, entry −2.5: 0.88, +7.0%, 57 trades).

A better risk-adjusted result than holding YM, but a small one. Holding YM made +19% over 2023–2024 (Sharpe 0.82, drawdown −10%). The three best passers beat that Sharpe with drawdowns of −2% to −3%, but made only +5% to +10%. Passers were in the market about 13% of the time (median).

Weak support from ES. 8 of the 9 passers made money with the same rules on ES over 2018–2022, but the median ES Sharpe was only 0.14 (NQ's passers: 0.49).

No single strong plateau. Around the top-ranked passer the heatmap shows a small patch (average 30 with volatility 15–25 scores 0.66–0.89) surrounded by weak cells. Every local nudge stayed at Sharpe 0.34 or above, so there's no cliff like ES.
