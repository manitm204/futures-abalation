  • Up-bar confirmation is the biggest lever: 0.32 against 0.01 without it.
  • Averages 25–45 scored best (0.29–0.34) and the score fades steadily from 75 to 115 (0.17 down to 0.05). Hold 3 was weakest (0.09).
  • Volatility, entry, exit and stop made little difference (all 0.08–0.26).
  • 634 versions cleared the profit gate, and 626 passed the stable-settings test.

56 of 324 finalists passed 2023–2024 (35 of 176 groups). 130 of 324 made money, and the median finalist's Sharpe was −0.15. The median passer made +4.3%, Sharpe 0.59, drawdown −3.3%, 57 trades, about 9% time in market. 32 failed for having fewer than 40 trades.

  • The passers lean to EMA (42 of 56), up-bar confirmation (48), entry −1.5 (44) and hold 5 (35). Most use the long averages 75–115, though on 2018–2022 the short ones scored better.
