Same test as the other markets, on gold. Same 41,472-version v5 grid and funnel, with validation needing ≥ 40 trades. Differences:

  • Session: the COMEX day session, 08:20–13:30 New York (the 13:30 settlement), so a 1-hour day is 5 bars plus a 10-minute stub.
  • No cross-market check, since no related market (such as silver) is in the data yet.
  • 35 stale days skipped. At some month-ends Databento's front-month series stays on the expiring contract for a day or two, trading about 1% of normal volume (e.g. 208 contracts on 2018-01-31 against ~120,000 normally). Days with under 5% of the previous 20 days' median volume are not traded. The same rule removes nothing on ES, NQ, YM or RTY.

GC rolls about every two months (44 rolls, back-adjusted).

1-hour bars dominate. On 2018–2022, 61% of 1-hour versions made money, against 17% of 15-minute and 6% of 5-minute ones. Bar size mattered far more than any other setting. Up-bar confirmation helped across the grid, but most 1-hour finalists use no confirmation and a 2 × ATR stop.

Most stable development results so far. 177 versions cleared the profit test, and 162 of them (92%) also held up when each setting was nudged. Drawdowns were small: the 89 finalists' median 2018–2022 drawdown was −5.7%, for a median +21% and Sharpe 0.84.

24 of 89 finalists passed 2023–2024 (15 groups). The median finalist was flat (Sharpe 0.00), and 44 of 89 made money. 12 more fell short only on trade count (under 40 trades). For the first time, the 2018–2022 ranking pointed the right way, although weakly (rank correlation +0.24; it was negative on CL, YM and NQ v6).

The passers are mostly one family: 1-hour bars, average 50–60, volatility 15–20, entry −2.5 to −3, no confirmation, 2 × ATR stop. The best:

  • #23: SMA 60/15, entry −3, exit 0, hold 10. Sharpe 1.29, +14.2%, drawdown −5.1%, 61 trades.
  • #41 and #20: EMA 60/20, entry −2.5, hold 10. Sharpe 1.16–1.19, +11–12%, drawdown −4.9%.
  • #7 and #64: SMA 60/15, entry −3, exit 0.5, with or without the stop. Sharpe 1.12–1.13, +12%.

A smaller 15-minute EMA 20–40 / volatility 15 family with up-bar confirmation also passed, but more weakly (Sharpe 0.38–0.80, +1–3%).

Holding gold did better in raw return. Gold rallied in 2023–2024: holding GC (rolled, unlevered) made +29.7% (Sharpe 1.00, drawdown −13%). The best passers made about half that, but with a third of the drawdown and in the market only 15–20% of the time. Their Sharpe (up to 1.29) matches or beats holding.

Fairly broad plateau. Around #3 (1-hour EMA 50, volatility 20), volatility 15–20 with average 40–80 scores Sharpe 0.45–1.16. Volatility 25 and above falls off quickly.
