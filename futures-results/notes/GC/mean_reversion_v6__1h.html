GC round 2, 1-hour half. 17,280 versions, specified by you after reading GC v5:

  • SMA and EMA; averages 30/40/50/60/80; volatility 12/15/18/20/22/25.
  • Entry −2/−2.5/−3, exit −0.5/0/+0.5, hold 5/8/10/14, confirmation none or up bar, stop none/1×/1.5×/2× ATR.

Same rules as GC v5. GC 2023–2024 is partly in-sample here, since the v5 results shaped the grid.

On 2018–2022 the 1-hour grid is broadly positive. 83% of versions made money (median Sharpe 0.34).

  • Volatility 18–25 was best (0.41–0.42); 12 was weak (0.09).
  • Averages 40–50 scored best (0.41–0.45), and 80 was weakest (0.21). Hold 5 was weak (0.20) against 0.35–0.39 for 8–14.
  • A 1.5× or 2× stop beat 1× (0.37–0.42 against 0.24). Up-bar confirmation helped a little (0.39 against 0.29).
  • 838 versions cleared the profit gate, and 808 passed the stable-settings test.

72 of 282 finalists passed 2023–2024 (46 of 149 groups). But only 119 of 282 made money, and the median finalist's Sharpe was −0.05. The median passer made +5.2%, Sharpe 0.61, drawdown −4.5%, 55 trades. 18 failed for having fewer than 40 trades.

  • The passers almost all skip the confirmation (69 of 72), even though it helped on 2018–2022. Most use averages 50–60 (55), volatility 15–22 (70), exit +0.5 (48) and a 1.5× stop (35).
  • The best 2023–2024 results were average 60 with volatility 15–22: #33 (SMA 60/15, entry −3, exit −0.5, hold 14, 1.5× stop) made +13.1%, Sharpe 1.23, on 68 trades. #197 (EMA 60/15, entry −2.5, exit 0, hold 10, 1.5× stop) made +13.2%, Sharpe 1.22, on 78 trades.

The 2018–2022 ranking didn't predict 2023–2024 (rank correlation −0.09). The top two failed; #3 and #5 passed.

Mostly not better than luck. The median finalist beat only 27% of random-entry runs on Sharpe and the median passer 65%. 4 passers beat 90%+: #197 (95%), #33 (93%), and #160 and #170 (one group, 91%). Holding gold made +29.7% over 2023–2024 (Sharpe 1.00), more than double the best passer.
