Same test as ES v5. Same 41,472-version grid and funnel: local-nudge stability (≥ 70% profitable, median Sharpe ≥ 0.5), best 2 per duplicate group, 1.5× cost stress, and validation needing net > 0, Sharpe ≥ 0.30, drawdown no worse than 30% and at least 40 trades. The cross-market check runs on ES.

Mean reversion works far better on NQ than on ES. On 2018–2022, 40% of 1-hour, 35% of 15-minute and 20% of 5-minute versions made money (ES: 45%, 14%, 3%). 190 versions cleared the profit test (ES: 88), and 93 finalists from 55 groups went to validation.

38 of 93 passed 2023–2024, from 25 different groups. 24 are 1-hour and 14 are 15-minute; no 5-minute version passed. Across all 93 finalists the median 2023–2024 Sharpe was 0.42 and 61 made money (ES: 0.02, and 24 of 45). 35 of the 38 passers also made money with the same rules on ES over 2018–2022 (median Sharpe 0.49).

The clearest family: 1-hour, short volatility length, entry −2, up-bar confirmation. The top passers are 1-hour EMA 30 with volatility length 10, entry −2 and a 1–2 × ATR stop. #5 made Sharpe 1.55 and +17.1%, #4 1.27 and +14.7%, #32 1.72 and +15.2% and #53 1.56 and +17.6% on 2023–2024 (41–60 trades, drawdown −3% to −5%). 33 of the 38 passers use the up-bar confirmation, and 25 use volatility length 10 or 15.

Unlike ES, the winners sit on a plateau. Every local nudge of #4 kept its 2018–2022 Sharpe at 0.60 or above (ES's survivor fell to −0.56 when volatility length moved from 20 to 16). In the heatmap, average 30–40 with volatility 10–15 forms a green patch rather than a single cell. One caution: volatility length 10 is the shortest value in the grid. The nudge to 8 still scores 0.91, but nothing shorter was tested.

Still far behind just holding NQ. The passers made +2% to +18% over 2023–2024 (median +5.8%) while in the market about 7% of the time. Holding NQ made +74% (Sharpe 1.64), with a −14% drawdown against −2% to −8% for the passers. As a standalone account it's a low-exposure, low-drawdown add-on rather than a return engine.

Cleaner than ES, but not untouched. NQ 2023–2024 was never used to pick anything. But the v5 grid and the 40-trade rule were chosen after reading ES 2023–2024 results, NQ is highly correlated with ES, and NQ 2018–2022 was used as ES's cross-check. The 2025+ holdout has not been run.
