NQ squeeze round 3, 5-minute bars. 27,648 versions, specified at the same time as the 15-minute grid, after reading the ES squeeze rounds' 2018–2022, 2023–2024 and 2025+ results:

  • Squeeze length 1/3/5/10/15/30 bars, Keltner width 0.5/1/1.5/2/2.5/3 ATR, at least 1/4 squeeze bars in a row. With length 1, almost every bar counts as a squeeze bar, so the box is the session's range so far.
  • Wick entry only; the box stays live 1/3/10 bars.
  • Stop at the box or 1/2/3 ATR; target 2R/3R/4R/none; long or both ways; EMA(50) filter on/off; minimum box width any/0.5 ATR.

2023–2024 is a clean test on NQ. No squeeze strategy had been scored on NQ 2023–2024 before this round. The 2025+ holdout is untouched. The first attempt at this run was cut short by a computer crash during stage 7; it was re-run from the saved 2018–2022 backtests with identical settings.

Most versions made money on 2018–2022. 69% did, and 4,488 cleared the profit gate (Sharpe ≥ 0.75). That is more than 2,000, so the prescreen kept the best 3 of each group (858 versions). What helped:

  • The stop mattered most: a 1-ATR stop was only 35% profitable (median Sharpe −0.21), against 82–85% for the box or a 2-ATR stop.
  • The EMA(50) filter (80% profitable vs 58%) and a Keltner width of 1 or 1.5 ATR (80–84%, against 54% at 0.5 and 60% at 3).
  • 1 squeeze bar over 4 (75% vs 63%), and wider targets (no target 76%, 2R 58%). Squeeze length, setup expiry and minimum box width made little difference.

443 finalists from 225 groups, all of which survived 1.5× costs.

301 of 443 passed 2023–2024, from 154 groups. The median passer made +11.1% over two years at Sharpe 0.97, profit factor 1.15, drawdown −5.1%. Across all 443 the median was +7.4%, Sharpe 0.71, profit factor 1.10. 142 finalists had a 2023–2024 Sharpe of 1.0 or more.

  • Against the 15-minute round: higher return and Sharpe (median +7.4% / 0.71 vs +4.8% / 0.64), but a lower profit factor (1.10 vs 1.18), deeper drawdowns (−5.6% vs −4.1%), more time in the market (25% vs 15%) and more than twice the trades. Each trade earns less, so costs and fills matter more here.
  • Holding NQ made +74% (Sharpe 1.64, drawdown −14%).
  • The 2018–2022 ranking said little about 2023–2024 (rank correlation 0.10). The 2018–2022 leader (#2: length 15, width 1, box stop, 2R, both ways, EMA filter, dev Sharpe 1.99) made almost nothing (Sharpe 0.05).
  • Long-only did much better than long-and-short (155 of 211 passed, median Sharpe 0.97, against 146 of 232 and 0.54), and the box stop beat the ATR stops (141 of 197 passed). Length 30 fell apart (23 of 91 passed); length 3 did best (66 of 79, median Sharpe 1.04).

The best versions clearly beat random entries. The median finalist beat 82% of random-entry runs on Sharpe (89% among the passers); 78 beat 95% and 32 beat 99%.

  • #202 (length 15, width 2.5, live 10 bars, box stop, 4R target, long, EMA filter, 0.5-ATR box): +22%, Sharpe 2.03, profit factor 1.57, drawdown −3.5%, in the market 16% of the time, beat 99% of random runs. #185 is the same with the box live 1 bar.
  • #5 (length 3, width 0.5, live 1 bar, box stop, 4R target, long, EMA filter): +25%, Sharpe 1.98, profit factor 1.28, drawdown −3.4%, beat 98%. It was also near the top on 2018–2022 (dev Sharpe 1.61).
