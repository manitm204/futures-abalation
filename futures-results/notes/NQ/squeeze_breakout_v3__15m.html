NQ squeeze round 3, 15-minute bars. 28,800 versions, specified after reading the ES squeeze rounds' 2018–2022, 2023–2024 and 2025+ results. This is the first squeeze run on NQ itself:

  • Squeeze length 5/15/30/50/70 bars (30 and longer reach into the previous session), Keltner width 1/1.5/2/2.5/3 ATR, at least 1/3 squeeze bars in a row.
  • Close or wick entry; the box stays live 1/3/7 bars.
  • Stop at the box or 1/2 ATR; target 1.5R/2.5R/3R/none; long or both ways; EMA(50) filter on/off; minimum box width any/0.5 ATR.

2023–2024 is a clean test on NQ. No squeeze strategy had been scored on NQ 2023–2024. The ES runs read NQ 2018–2022 only, as a transfer check. The 2025+ holdout is untouched. The 5-minute run of this round is the other variant of this tab.

Most versions made money on 2018–2022. 72% did, and 5,304 cleared the profit gate (Sharpe ≥ 0.75). That is more than 2,000, so the prescreen kept the best 3 of each group (1,080 versions). What helped:

  • A Keltner width of 1.5 ATR or more: 1.0 ATR was only 39% profitable, against 75–85% for the wider settings.
  • A close entry (82% profitable) over a wick entry (62%), and the box stop (80%) over a 1- or 2-ATR stop (66–69%).
  • The EMA(50) filter (76% vs 68%) and 1 squeeze bar over 3 (78% vs 66%). Squeeze length, target, setup expiry, direction and minimum box width made little difference.

567 finalists from 288 groups.

439 of 567 passed 2023–2024, from 229 groups. The median passer made +6.7% over two years at Sharpe 0.81, profit factor 1.23, drawdown −3.9%. Across all 567 the median was +4.8%, Sharpe 0.64, profit factor 1.18. 139 finalists had a 2023–2024 Sharpe of 1.0 or more.

  • Holding NQ made +74% (Sharpe 1.64, drawdown −14%). The best finalists earned a quarter to a half of that, at a sixth of the drawdown or less.
  • The 2018–2022 ranking said nothing about 2023–2024 (rank correlation −0.02). The 2018–2022 leaders (#1/#2: length 50, width 3, both ways, no filter, dev Sharpe 1.72) fell to Sharpe 0.32.
  • Long-only did better than long-and-short (185 of 219 passed, median Sharpe 0.79, against 254 of 348 and 0.55), and close entries did better than wick entries (272 of 321 against 167 of 246). Length 5 passed most often (92 of 106).

The best versions clearly beat random entries. The median finalist beat 76% of random-entry runs on Sharpe; 78 beat 95% and 24 beat 99%.

  • #327 (length 5, width 1.5, close entry, live 3 bars, box stop, no target, long, EMA filter): +24%, Sharpe 1.89, profit factor 1.57, drawdown −2.4%, beat 99%.
