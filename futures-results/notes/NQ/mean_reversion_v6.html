What changed from NQ v5. Only the grid, which now zooms in on what passed NQ v5: average length 20–50, volatility length 5–25 (adding 5 and 8), entry −2 to −3.5, exit −1.25 to +0.5, max hold 3/5/10, up-bar confirmation always on, and stop none or 1.5 × ATR. That makes 21,600 versions. The funnel is unchanged.

Read this first: 2023–2024 is no longer a fair test for NQ. This grid was built around the versions that passed NQ's 2023–2024 validation in v5. A high pass rate here is largely the result of that choice, not new evidence. Only the 2025+ holdout can confirm it.

161 of 201 finalists passed 2023–2024, from 90 of 109 groups. The median finalist's 2023–2024 Sharpe was 0.94, and 191 of 201 made money. Passers made +2% to +28% (median +9.8%) with median drawdown −4.3% and about 54 trades, while in the market about 8% of the time. 157 of the 161 also made money with the same rules on ES over 2018–2022 (median Sharpe 0.47). 24 finalists failed only for having fewer than 40 trades.

1-hour bars dominate. On 2018–2022, 60% of 1-hour versions made money (median Sharpe 0.26), against 30% on 15-minute and 9% on 5-minute bars. 173 of the 201 finalists and 143 of the 161 passers are 1-hour.

Volatility 8–10 is a real plateau. Around the top-ranked finalist (#4: 1-hour SMA 30, volatility 8, entry −3.5, hold 5, 1.5 × ATR stop), average length 25–50 with volatility 8 or 10 scores 0.64–1.25 on 2018–2022. That's a broad patch, unlike ES's single column. Volatility 15 and above drops off.

The 2018–2022 ranking did not predict 2023–2024. Across the 201 finalists, the rank correlation between 2018–2022 and 2023–2024 Sharpe was −0.06. The 20 best-ranked had a median 2023–2024 Sharpe of 0.97, against 1.16 for the 20 worst-ranked. The top-ranked passers (#4–#7) made only Sharpe 0.51–0.69, while the best 2023–2024 results (Sharpe 1.9–2.05, about +20%) came from versions ranked 54th to 188th. So the region works, but picking "the best" version within it is luck.

Still behind holding NQ. Holding NQ made +74% over 2023–2024 (Sharpe 1.64). The median passer matched about 0.6 of that Sharpe with a third of the drawdown, at roughly 8% time in market.
