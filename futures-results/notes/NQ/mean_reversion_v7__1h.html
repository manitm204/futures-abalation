On 2018–2022 short volatility lengths are the plateau. 54% of versions made money (median Sharpe 0.16).

  • Volatility 6–12 scored median Sharpe 0.41–0.56. From 30 up it turns negative.
  • A stop helps: 1.5× and 2× ATR scored 0.23–0.24, no stop 0.05. Only 2 of the 214 stable versions have no stop.
  • SMA beat EMA (0.26 against 0.04), and entry −2 beat −2.5 and −3.
  • All 214 versions that cleared the profit gate also passed the stable-settings test.

123 of 153 finalists passed 2023–2024, from 71 of 83 groups. 147 of 153 made money, and the median finalist's Sharpe was 0.92. The median passer made +10.6%, Sharpe 0.96, drawdown −5.1%, 57 trades, about 11% time in market. 20 failed for having fewer than 40 trades.

  • Every passer uses a stop (71 at 1.5×, 52 at 2×). Most use volatility 6–12 and exit 0.
  • The best 2023–2024 results were SMA 28 with volatility 6–12 and a 2× ATR stop. #119 (SMA 28/12, entry −2, exit 0, hold 12) made Sharpe 1.75, +21.1%. #89 (SMA 28/6) made +29.9%, Sharpe 1.64, on 92 trades.

The 2018–2022 ranking didn't predict 2023–2024 (rank correlation −0.06). The top-ranked #1 (SMA 32/12, entry −2.5) lost money, while the best results came from versions ranked 64th to 135th. As in v6, the region works but picking the best version inside it is luck.

Better than luck, more often than elsewhere. The median finalist beat 75% of random-entry runs on Sharpe, and 10 of 153 beat 95%+.
