Same test as ES, NQ and YM v5. Same 41,472-version grid and funnel, validation needing ≥ 40 trades, cross-market check on ES.

The weakest of the four equity-index markets. On 2018–2022, 33% of 1-hour, 31% of 15-minute and 8% of 5-minute versions made money. Only 69 versions cleared the profit test, and 27 finalists from 16 groups went to validation.

5 of 27 passed 2023–2024, and they were the only finalists that made money. The median finalist's 2023–2024 Sharpe was −0.17, and just 5 of 27 were profitable. Those 5 are the passers, from 3 groups and all with volatility length 15. Four are 1-hour versions with average length 80, entry −3 and no stop (Sharpe 0.34–0.60, +4.6% to +8.0%, about 51 trades, drawdown about −8%). The other is a 15-minute SMA 50 with up-bar confirmation (Sharpe 0.62, +6.0%, 203 trades).

Little sign of a real edge.

  • None of the 5 passers made money with the same rules on ES over 2018–2022 (ES Sharpe −0.65 to −0.02).
  • Four of them use average length 80, the longest value in the grid, so the other side of that setting was never tested.
  • The heatmap around the top-ranked passer is scattered: green cells sit next to red ones rather than forming a patch.

Compared with holding RTY. Holding RTY made +17% over 2023–2024 (Sharpe 0.48, drawdown −20%). The passers made +5% to +8% with drawdowns of −4% to −9%, so they were roughly level on Sharpe.

Not fully clean. The v5 grid and 40-trade rule were chosen after ES results; RTY 2023–2024 was never used to pick anything. The 2025+ holdout has not been run.
