Round 2 on the Russell, with a grid you specified after v5. Grid v8, 84,672 versions:

  • 15-minute and 1-hour bars; SMA and EMA; averages 20–100.
  • Volatility 10/15/20 and 60–120; entry −2.5 to −4; exit −1.25 to +1.
  • Hold 3/5/8; confirmation none/up bar; stop none/1×/2× ATR.

Every rule is the same as RTY v5 (stable-settings test, best 2 per group, validation ≥ 40 trades, ES cross-check). RTY 2023–2024 is partly in-sample here, because this grid was chosen after seeing v5's validation results.

The long volatility lengths were the big change. On 2018–2022:

  • Volatility 60–120 made money in 40–45% of versions, against 26–33% for 10–20.
  • Volatility 80 reached Sharpe 0.75 most often (7.6% of versions).
  • Unlike v5, no confirmation beat up-bar confirmation (52% profitable against 24%), and no stop beat a 1 × ATR stop.
  • Exit level barely mattered.

Deep entries trade rarely: only 35% of versions had enough trades to qualify.

19 of 108 finalists passed 2023–2024 (12 groups). The median finalist was slightly negative (Sharpe −0.08), and 40 of 108 made money. 26 failed on trade count alone. As on most markets, the 2018–2022 ranking pointed the wrong way (rank correlation −0.25).

Average 100, the longest tested, again dominates. 15 of the 19 passers use it. In v5 the passers sat at average 80, also the grid edge, so the edge has moved out with the grid, which is a warning sign. The passers split into two families:

  • 1-hour SMA 100, short volatility (10–15), hold 8. The best was #60 (volatility 15, entry −3, exit +0.5, no stop): Sharpe 1.27, +16.6%, drawdown −4.2%, 47 trades. #62 (exit +1) is nearly identical. Both are flat on ES (Sharpe 0.0), so the edge doesn't transfer.
  • 15-minute EMA/SMA 100, long volatility (60–100), short holds. Smoother but tiny, since they're in the market only 1–5% of the time. #20 (EMA 100/80, entry −3, hold 3, 1 × ATR stop) made Sharpe 0.84 but only +3.6%. This family does transfer to ES (Sharpe 0.44–0.47).

Stability looks good, but only on 2018–2022. Around #3 (15-minute EMA 100/80), volatility 60–100 scores Sharpe 0.6–1.4 at almost every average length, a broad plateau. Volatility 10–20 is mostly negative.

Compared with holding RTY: holding made +16.8% over 2023–2024 (Sharpe 0.48, drawdown −20%). #60 matched the return with a Sharpe of 1.27 and a fifth of the drawdown. The 15-minute family made far less.

The 2025+ holdout has not been run.
