RTY final round, 1-hour half. 8,640 versions, specified by you after reading RTY v5 and v6:

  • SMA and EMA; averages 70–120 in steps of 10; volatility 10/12/15/18/20.
  • Entry −2.5/−3, exit −1/0/+1, hold 5/8/12, confirmation none or up bar, stop none/1.5×/2×/2.5× ATR.

Same rules as RTY v6. RTY 2023–2024 is largely in-sample here, since the v5 and v6 results shaped the grid.

On 2018–2022 the 1-hour grid is broadly positive. 68% of versions made money (median Sharpe 0.22).

  • Averages 90–110 scored best (0.28–0.32), and 70 was flat (0.01). Volatility 12–15 beat the rest (0.31–0.35).
  • Hold 8–12 beat hold 5 (0.26–0.27 against 0.11). Up-bar confirmation helped a little (0.27 against 0.18), and no stop scored slightly best (0.28).
  • 299 versions cleared the profit gate, and 280 passed the stable-settings test.

69 of 162 finalists passed 2023–2024 (41 of 90 groups). 102 of 162 made money, and the median finalist's Sharpe was 0.20. The median passer made +9.9%, Sharpe 0.69, drawdown −7.4%, 52 trades, about 15% time in market.

  • The passers mostly use no confirmation (51 of 69), hold 8–12 (64), exit 0 or +1 (58), volatility 12–15 (54) and averages 90–110 (55).
  • The best 2023–2024 results were EMA 100–110 with volatility 12, entry −2.5, hold 12, no confirmation. #47 (EMA 110/12, exit +1, 2× ATR stop) made +29.2%, Sharpe 1.69, on 74 trades. #110 (same with a 1.5× stop) made +25.7%, Sharpe 1.46, on 88 trades. #154 (exit 0, hold 8, no stop) made Sharpe 1.52, +19.1%.

The 2018–2022 ranking didn't predict 2023–2024 (rank correlation −0.07). The top-ranked #1 passed, but weakly (Sharpe 0.32).

Better than luck more often than any other RTY run. The median finalist beat 59% of random-entry runs on Sharpe and the median passer 83%. 23 passers beat 90%+, and #47 and #92 beat 100%. Holding RTY made +16.8% over 2023–2024 (Sharpe 0.48), and many passers beat that. This grid was built around RTY's own 2023–2024 results, though, so only the 2025+ holdout can confirm it.
