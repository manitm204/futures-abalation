First squeeze run on RTY; 2023–2024 is a clean test here and the 2025+ holdout is untouched.

Plenty of finalists, but most fell apart on 2023–2024. 53% of versions made money on 2018–2022 (median Sharpe 0.07) and 369 cleared the profit gate (Sharpe ≥ 0.75), giving 215 finalists from 116 groups. What helped on 2018–2022: a wick entry (67% profitable vs 38% for close), 1 squeeze bar over 4 (70% vs 36%), longer squeezes (45 bars: 66%, against 36% at 15) and trading both ways (61% vs 45% long-only).

Only 17 of 215 passed 2023–2024, from 11 groups. The median finalist lost 4.1% over two years (Sharpe −0.38, profit factor 0.91); the median passer made +6.1% (Sharpe 0.78, profit factor 1.28, drawdown −2.9%). Holding RTY made +17% (Sharpe 0.48, drawdown −20%).
