ES squeeze round 2, 15-minute bars. 184,320 versions, specified after reading round 1's 2018–2022 and 2023–2024 results:

  • Squeeze length 5/10/15/20/25/30/40/50 bars, Keltner width 1.0/1.5/2.0/2.5/3.0 ATR, at least 1/3/5 squeeze bars in a row.
  • Close or wick entry; the box stays live 1/3/6/9 bars.
  • Stop at the box or 1.5/2.5/3.5 ATR; target 2R/3R/4R/none; long or both ways; EMA(50) filter on/off; minimum box width any/0.5/1.0 ATR.

2023–2024 is not clean for this round. The grid was chosen after seeing round 1 on 2023–2024, so passing validation here is weaker evidence than in round 1. The 2025+ holdout is untouched.

Most versions still lose on 2018–2022. 46% made money (median Sharpe −0.02), and 8,524 cleared the profit gate (Sharpe ≥ 0.75). What helped:

  • Long squeezes: length 40 and 50 were 75–76% profitable, lengths 10–25 only 19–38%.
  • One squeeze bar is enough: 63% profitable, against 36–38% when 3 or 5 in a row were required.
  • Wick entries over closes (53% vs 38%), and a tight stop (1.5 ATR or the box, 51–57%) over a wide one (2.5/3.5 ATR, 37–38%).
  • No trend filter did slightly better than EMA(50) (50% vs 41%).
  • The target barely mattered: 2R or more is rarely reached before the close (a 2R target closed 8% of trades), so most trades end at the stop or the session close. Setup expiry and minimum box width made no difference.

418 finalists from 214 groups.

285 of 418 passed 2023–2024, from 149 groups. The median passer made +5.1% over two years at Sharpe 0.83, profit factor 1.26, drawdown −3.2%. Across all 418 the median was +3.7%, Sharpe 0.63, profit factor 1.18. 102 finalists had a 2023–2024 Sharpe of 1.0 or more.

  • Holding ES made +41% (Sharpe 1.40, drawdown −11%). The best finalists earned about half of that, at under half the drawdown.
  • Tight squeezes still failed: 6 of 16 finalists with width 1.0 passed, against 119 of 172 at 2.0.
  • Long-only passed more often (154 of 195) than long-and-short (131 of 223).
  • The 2018–2022 ranking said almost nothing about 2023–2024 (rank correlation 0.06), so which finalist ends up on top is largely luck of the draw.

The best versions clearly beat random entries. The median finalist beat 84% of random-entry runs on Sharpe; 103 beat 95% and 34 beat 99%. Random long-only trades did slightly better than random two-way trades in 2023–2024 (median Sharpe 0.18 vs −0.33).
