Volatility 21 is a knife edge, not a plateau. On 2018–2022:

  • 89% of volatility-21 versions made money (median Sharpe 0.42).
  • One step either side drops sharply: 18 → 52%, 24 → 55%, and 12–15 → 6–10%.
  • Average length mattered much less (42–57 all fine, 32 weak).
