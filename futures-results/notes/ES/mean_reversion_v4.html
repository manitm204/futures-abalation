What changed from v3. Only the stable-settings test (stage 4). v3 moved each setting one full grid step (e.g. average length 40 → 12, bar size 15min → 5min) and needed a median neighbor Sharpe ≥ 0.30. v4 nudges each setting slightly, one at a time: lengths and max hold ±20%, entry/exit z ±0.25 and a numeric ATR stop ±0.25. It needs ≥ 70% of the nudged versions profitable, with median Sharpe ≥ 0.50. Everything else is identical, and stages 1–3 reuse v3's backtests.

The new test is more forgiving, not stricter. 55 of the 57 profit-test survivors passed (v3: 24), because small nudges usually leave results close to the original. After removing duplicates, 18 finalists went to validation (v3: 11).

3 of 18 passed 2023–2024, but only two different ideas. #1 and #2 are near-twins (same group, 15-minute SMA 40 with volatility 20, entry −3, hold 5, up-bar confirmation, 2.5 × ATR stop; only the exit differs). They made Sharpe 1.13 and 1.03 on 2023–2024 (+5.1% and +4.8%, 70 and 69 trades) and 0.89 and 0.79 on NQ over 2018–2022. #15 (entry −2.5, exit −1) passed with Sharpe 0.35. v3's grid-neighbor test had rejected #1, because full-step moves like 15min → 5min lost money.

#1 has a cliff the gate doesn't see. Nudging its volatility length from 20 to 16 drops the 2018–2022 Sharpe to −0.61, and to 24 gives −0.21. It passes only because the other 10 nudges are fine, and the gate looks at the share and median, not at any single setting. See the local-nudge table below.

1-hour versions failed again. All 9 one-hour finalists failed validation, as in v3. v3's survivor (15-min EMA, hold 16) passes the new stage 4 but now loses its group's top-2 spot to hold-5 versions, which failed validation (Sharpe 0.24 and 0.27).

Still small and rarely in the market. The passers held a position about 3–5% of the time. They made about +5% over 2023–2024 against +40.7% for holding ES, with a drawdown of about −2% against −11.3%.

Treat this pass with extra caution. This test was chosen after seeing v3's 2023–2024 results, so ES 2023–2024 is no longer clean for this strategy. That is recorded in the run's config. The 2025+ holdout is now the only untouched check.
