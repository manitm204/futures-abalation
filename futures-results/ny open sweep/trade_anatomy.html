NY-open sweep · trade anatomy · 2018–2024 (development + validation, no holdout)

How NQ #64 and GC #7 actually make money

Every trade, measured in R: profit or loss divided by the risk at entry (entry to stop). −1R is a full stop-out; +3R is three times the risk. R puts NQ and gold on the same scale. Dollar figures are for one micro contract (MNQ $2/pt, MGC $10/pt) after costs.

1 · Distribution

Every trade's result

Log scale, so the rare big winners are visible next to the stop-outs (the tall bar at −1R). Everything to the right of 0 is the trades that held the break until the time exit.

2 · Winners vs losers

Box plots: how big are the wins and losses?

Box = middle half of trades, line = median, whiskers = typical range, dots = every trade. The losers are a thin line because the stop caps them almost exactly.

3 · Dependence on big winners

What's left if the best trades never happened

Total profit (in R) after removing the N biggest winners. When a line crosses 0, the strategy's whole profit lives in those N trades.

4 · Over time

Cumulative R, trade by trade

One unit of risk per trade, no compounding. Flat or down-sloping stretches are the losing streaks you'd have to sit through.

5 · Inside the trade

How far trades go in your favour before they end

x = the best open profit the trade reached (MFE), y = how it ended. Dots near y = −1 far to the right are trades that were well in profit and then got stopped.
